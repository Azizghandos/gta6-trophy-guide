    Roadmap to the platinum

    PowerPyx rates the platinum 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours, in a single playthrough. There are no online trophies, no mission gold medals, and no mission replay — so play in this order, keep a save at the start of each chapter, and leave cheats off: cheats disable trophies and saving.

      About this guide

      Grand Theft Auto VI launched on 19 November 2026. This guide covers the official PlayStation 5 list — 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned — as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, who agree on every name, description and grade.

      Every guide is written in this entry’s own words. Where a fact comes from another guide, it is cited beside the trophy that uses it, with a link — a cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed. Five hidden trophies are still unsolved in the community guides (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin); their guides say so plainly instead of guessing.

        How to use this tracker

        • Tick a trophy when it pops; your progress is saved in this browser.
        • Spoiler-free mode hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.
        • Use Export progress to back up your ticks, and Import progress to restore them on another machine.
        • This app runs entirely offline: it loads nothing from the network.
